The Sun contains 99.86 percent of all the mass in our solar system.

Jupiter is the largest planet. It is so big that more than 1300 Earths could fit inside it.

A day on Venus is longer than its year. Venus spins so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one trip around the Sun.

Neptune was the first planet found using math instead of a telescope. Astronomers predicted its location from the way it tugged on Uranus.

Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.
